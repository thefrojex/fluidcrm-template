# FluidCRM (AppFoundry template)

A deals CRM with a timeline per company, reminders, and AI-suggested next steps - and a WhatsApp
assistant: workspace employees who are members of the app can log a client conversation, check
their agenda, complete events or update a deal by messaging the workspace's WhatsApp number.

- Sign in with your workspace login. The first person to sign in becomes the CRM's admin and adds
  everyone else on the Team page.
- Every login gets its own private board, filled with sample deals dated around today
  ("Reset demo data" in the account menu starts it over).
- Design and data docs: `docs/`.
